About this reader

JL8 is a webcomic by Yale Stewart — the Justice League reimagined as eight-year-olds. It ran for 270 entries.

This is an unofficial, fan-made reader built to be faster and friendlier than flipping pages one server round-trip at a time: the whole catalog is indexed up front, so navigation is instant, comics preload as you read, and your place is remembered.
